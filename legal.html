📜 Patent Considerations

Most apps don't need patents. Know when they matter.

What's Patentable

  • Utility patents: Novel, non-obvious processes (unique algorithms, ML methods, hardware integration)
  • Design patents: Unique ornamental UI/UX designs (15 years protection)
  • NOT patentable: Abstract ideas, basic CRUD apps, standard UI patterns, business methods alone

Cost/Benefit Analysis

TypeCost (DIY)Cost (Attorney)DurationBest For
Provisional$75-300$1,500-3,0001 year ("Patent Pending")Buying time, fundraising
Non-provisional (Utility)$5,000-10,000$10,000-20,000+20 years from filingCore novel tech
Design Patent$500-1,000$2,000-4,00015 years from grantUnique UI design
Maintenance Fees$400 (3.5yr), $900 (7.5yr), $1,850 (11.5yr), $3,700 (15.5yr)

When to File

✅ File if: Truly novel algorithm/process, unique hardware integration, defensible competitive moat, budget > $15K
❌ Skip if: Standard SaaS features, UI patterns already in use, budget < $10K, "AI wrapper" apps

Alternative: Trade Secrets

  • No registration cost, lasts indefinitely
  • Requires: NDAs, access controls, documentation, employee agreements
  • Lost if: reverse-engineered, independently discovered, publicly disclosed
  • Best for: proprietary algorithms, user data models, business processes

🛡️ Competitor IP Risks

Don't get sued by the big players. Know the landscape.

Before Building - Freedom to Operate (FTO)

  1. Search Google Patents for your core features
  2. Search USPTO for assignee = major competitors (Apple, Google, Meta, Microsoft, Adobe, etc.)
  3. Check if your "novel" feature is actually patented by someone else
  4. Document your independent development (dated notes, commits)

Common Patent Minefields

  • In-app purchase flows: Apple, Google have extensive portfolios
  • Social features: Meta (friend graphs, feeds, stories)
  • Mobile sync: Dropbox, Google, Microsoft
  • AR/ML on device: Apple (CoreML, ARKit), Google (ML Kit)
  • Push notifications: Multiple holders

Defensive Strategies

  • Design around: Build differently, not just "similar"
  • Prior art: Publish your approach (blog, GitHub) to block others' patents
  • Defensive publication: Use Defensive Publications or IP.com
  • Join LOT Network / OIN: Patent non-aggression pacts (free for startups)
  • Insurance: IP infringement insurance (~$2-5K/year for $1M coverage)
💡 Reality Check: Most indie apps fly under the radar. Big companies sue when you're a threat ($10M+ ARR) or doing something identical to their core IP. Focus on differentiation, not paranoia.
