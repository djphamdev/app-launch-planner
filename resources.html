🎯 Choosing Your Stack

  • Solo dev, iOS first: SwiftUI + SwiftData (modern, productive)
  • Solo dev, both platforms: Flutter (single codebase, great tooling) or React Native + Expo (if you know JS)
  • Team with web devs: React Native + Expo (shared skills)
  • Performance-critical (games, AR): Native (Swift/Kotlin) or Unity/Godot
  • Budget < $10K, need both stores fast: Flutter or Expo

🎯 Paywall Best Practices (From Video Research)

  • Show paywall Day 0: 90%+ of trials start on first open
  • Hard paywall in fitness/health: Test removing "X" button
  • Button text: iOS = "Continue", Android = "Start free trial"
  • Winning copy: "Unlock lifetime insights" beats "Get all features" (4/5 tests)
  • Video on paywall: 2.5% → 4.7% → 6.8% conversion progression
  • Social proof: "Join 10,000+ users" above the fold
  • 3 plans optimal: Weekly + Monthly + Annual (with trial on weekly)
