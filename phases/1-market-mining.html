1Market Mining

Find app niches where people are already paying. Start with the market — not a flashy original idea. Improve something people already buy, for a narrower audience, with a recurring job.

Checkpoint 1A: Identify 3 app categories with $10K+ monthly revenue potential.

Solving the Core Problem

New app founders fail because they:

  • Build on assumptions instead of market data
  • Enter broad, competitive categories without a niche
  • Don't validate demand before months of development

This phase solves all three by giving you a systematic way to find profitable niches using REAL market data.

Step 2: Target Specific Audiences

Avoid broad categories. A generic fitness or notes app fights teams with years of brand. Narrow until the store listing makes it obvious who it's for — home workouts for older adults, not “fitness for everyone.” Personal messaging converts.

Proven profitable niches (from analysis):
  • 📱 Habit trackers for specific professions
  • 💰 Budget apps for side-hustlers
  • 🧘 Meditation for chronic pain sufferers
  • 🏋️ Strength tracking for teen athletes
  • 📖 Study tools for specific exam types

Step 3: Review Competitor Weaknesses

Key Insights from Video Transcripts

"Users aren't looking for something completely new. They're looking for something that solves the problem better."

Translation: Don't invent categories. Improve existing ones.

"Smaller niches are often a smarter place to start."

Generic fitness app = hard competition. Strength tracking for teenagers = defensible.
