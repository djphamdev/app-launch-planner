2Competitive Analysis

Analyze existing apps, find their weaknesses, and identify opportunities for your improved version.

Checkpoint 2A: You've completed Phase 1 and have 3 pain points identified.

The Problem

Most app founders see competitors and think "that's already crowded." But successful apps aren't invented—they're improved.

Step 1: Reverse-Engineer the Best Competitors

For each of your 3 target pain points, analyze the top 3-5 apps in that category:

  1. App Store Optimization - What keywords do they use?
  2. Pricing - What's their subscription model and price?
  3. Features - What do they offer vs what they don't?
  4. Reviews - Both positive and negative (the goldmine)

🛠 Research Tools

  • AppTweak / SensorTower - Keywords and ASO data
  • App Store Reviews (50 per app) - Organic user feedback
  • Apptrace - Download and revenue estimates

Step 2: Map Competitor Weaknesses

The secret sauce is treating negative reviews as free market research. Every complaint is an opportunity.

Common Competitor Weaknesses (from video analysis):
  • ❌ Onboarding friction - Hard sign-ups, too many steps
  • ❌ Paywall timing - Asking for money too early or too late
  • ❌ Feature bloat - Too many options, confusing UX
  • ❌ Generic messaging - "All-in-one" vs niche-focused
  • ❌ No mobile optimization - Clunky mobile experiences
  • ❌ Poor conversion optimization - Low free trial start rates

Step 3: Build Your Opportunity Matrix

Create a comparison table showing where existing apps fail and how you'll fix it:

Competitor Weakness Your Fix Conversion Boost
App A Hard to cancel trial One-click cancellations + clear reminders Reduce refunds by 40%
App B Too many features Simply cleaner, focused on core 3 features Increase onboarding speed 3x
Checkpoint 2B (Competitive Analysis):
  1. ✅ Analyze 3 competitor apps for your chosen niche
  2. ✅ List their top 5 user complaints
  3. ✅ Document 3 specific weaknesses you'll exploit
  4. ✅ Create your improvement strategy for each

Paywall Insights (Critical!)

From the paywall video, these patterns emerged:

  • Hard paywalls work in certain categories (fitness, health)
  • near 100% trial starts happen on Day 0 (first open)
  • Video on paywalls increases conversions (4.7% → 6.8%)
  • Social proof beats features ("Join 10,000+ users")
