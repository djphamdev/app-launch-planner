3Pain Point Validation

Test your app idea before building. Spend hours on validation, not months on wrong builds.

Checkpoint 3A: You've identified 1 specific app opportunity from Phase 1+2.

The Problem (Still)

You think you found a good idea. You're 90% likely wrong. Building before validation costs you $10K+ and months of time.

Why Build Before You Validate?

From the validation video:

"Building an app is usually the most expensive part of the process."

Building = months. Validation = hours.

The Validation Pyramid

Start cheap & light, escalate only if promising:

1️⃣ Search Intent

Are people searching for this?

  • Google Trends - Rising interest?
  • Keyword research - Search volume

2️⃣ Landing Page Test

Does it convert visitors?

  • Simple HTML page
  • CTA to "Join Waitlist"
  • Track bounce rate & clicks

3️⃣ Pre-orders

Will they pay now?

  • Stripe checkout button
  • Charge $5 for early access
  • 10+ orders = proceed
⚡ Quick Validation Checklist (Minimum Viable Test):
  1. Create a 1-page landing page in 2 hours
  2. Add price ($4.99/mo or $49/yr)
  3. Drive 20 visitors via Reddit/Quora/Facebook Groups
  4. If 5+ people click "Sign Up" → Strong signal
  5. If 1+ people pay → Validate with cash

Step 1: Check Search Demand

Find people already looking for your solution:

  • Google Trends — is interest growing?
  • Ubersuggest / Ahrefs — search volume for keywords
  • Reddit / Quora — are people asking these questions?
  • AnswerThePublic — free question mining

Step 2: Build a No-Code Validation Funnel

Use Carrd.co, Tally, or a simple HTML page. Don't overthink:

Page must include:
  • Hero: "Finally, a simpler way to [solve pain point]"
  • Price: "Only $X/month - cancel anytime"
  • CTA: "Join Waitlist" or "Get Early Access"
  • Countdown: "Launching [date]" to create urgency
Checkpoint 3B (Validation):
  1. ✅ Build a 1-page landing site
  2. ✅ Drive 20+ targeted visitors
  3. ✅ Track click-through rate (aim for 10%+)
  4. ✅ Get pre-orders or waitlist signups
  5. ✅ Document results: __ visitors, __ clicks, __ paid

Red Flags to Abort

Stop if you see:
  • 🔴 CTR < 2% on landing page
  • 🔴 0 pre-orders after 50+ visitors
  • 🔴 Competitors have 50K+ active users (too big to crack)
  • 🔴 The problem isn't recurring (one-time need)
