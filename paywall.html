Paywall Studio

Most buyers start a trial on day zero — before they use the app. Design the wall, the sequence, and the offer here. Autosaves in this browser.

1. First-open funnel

Nearly all trial starts happen the day they open the app. People who don’t try immediately rarely try at all. Push first-open → paywall view as close to 100% as you can.

2. Sequence

One fitness app flipped signup and paywall. Views went 40% → 94%. Trial starts +190%. Sales +52%. After the trial starts, force sign-in — Calm does this.

Onboarding → Hard paywall → Start trial → Then sign in
Onboarding → Hard signup → Paywall (if they survive)

Diminishing returns around 8 paywall views. Keep showing it — more views, more money — until that point.

3. Hard vs soft

Hard wall = no X. Soft = they can skip. Putting the X back once cut start-trial in half; trial-to-paid stayed the same, so revenue halved.

4. Explain the free trial

A three-screen explainer (try free → reminder 2 days before → timeline paywall) has lifted trial activations ~56%. A “resume” looking variant lost. Ask for notification permission on the reminder screen.

5. Copy + CTA lab

Sell the outcome, not the feature list. iOS likes Continue. Android likes Start free trial. “Unlock lifetime insights” beat “Get all features” on a one-time IAP.

Live wall

Plans above the fold. CTA above the fold. Extra context lives below for people who scroll when they have to pay.

6. Test the offer

Weekly vs yearly depends on category. Monthly is usually weak as the hero. Two or three plans convert; three often wins. Yearly alone can feel expensive — a $1.99/mo next to $10/yr makes $10 look cheap. Moving a 7-day trial off yearly onto a 3-day weekly 3x’d sales in one app.

7. Make value obvious

Graphics → real person → product video. One soft-paywall app moved 2.5% → 4.7% → 6.8%. A longer wall with plans on top and proof below has turned $200/day into $1,000/day at 15 downloads — and +50% sales just from more context, even on a visual emoji app.
