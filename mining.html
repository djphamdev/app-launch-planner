Review Mine

Don’t wait for a brilliant original idea. Start with markets people already pay in, narrow the audience, and steal the product spec from 1-star reviews. Autosaves locally.

Better, not new

Habit trackers, AI writers, meditation, calorie apps keep shipping because users want a version that feels built for them — easier, faster, cheaper, or more specific. Competition proves spend. Zero competitors is often a demand warning.

50-review worksheet

Negative reviews are free market research. Sort low → high. Recurring lines like “too expensive”, “confusing”, “too many ads” are the product. Three competitors, ~50 reviews each. You still don’t need to build yet.

Validate before you build

A landing page is hours. An app is months. Waitlist + demo video + cash beat “my friends said it sounds cool.” Opinion is not demand.
